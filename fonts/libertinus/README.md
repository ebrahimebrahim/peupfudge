# Libertinus Serif

`LibertinusSerif-Regular.otf` and `LibertinusSerif-Bold.otf` are unmodified
copies from the official [Libertinus 7.051 release](https://github.com/alerque/libertinus/releases/tag/v7.051).
They are byte-for-byte identical to the copies bundled with
[Typst assets 0.14.2](https://github.com/typst/typst-assets/tree/v0.14.2).
They were retrieved on 2026-08-04.
